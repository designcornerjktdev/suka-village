Scope: the Suka Village landing page (`app/page.tsx`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: the family trip organiser arranging a stay for an extended family, roughly 25 people at peak. Job: find out whether everyone will be occupied all day, whether the group fits, and how to ask. Action: one WhatsApp conversation. Proof on hand: the owner's published facility list (ATV, fishing pond, animal farm, jogging and bicycle tracks, pool, restaurant from the property's own garden), three units totalling seven bedrooms, the address and distances. No photography, no rates, no property-level reviews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Suka Village sells a day with things in it, not a room. The page reads as a day, in time order. It refuses the category's amenity-icon grid and floating booking bar — and it refuses the pinned reference's one weakness, the dead white gap between photographs.

OWN-WORLD: Paper white holds roughly 85% of every viewport. Forest green #0E4032 is the single committed colour: the palm mark, every hairline rule, the WhatsApp plate. Ink #1A1A17 for prose; secondary text tinted from the green, never gray. Marcellus in letterspaced caps for section titles, EB Garamond for prose and figures. Centered symmetric title blocks, hairline rules, full-bleed bands between vast breathing rooms. No cards anywhere, no eyebrows above headings, no icon grid.

STORY: An organiser arrives warm from Instagram carrying one fear — that everyone will be bored. They read the day and find it full. They see the compound holds the whole family. They open WhatsApp already knowing what they are booking.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White. Centered and small, the Suka Village palm mark in green. Beneath it SUKA VILLAGE in letterspaced Marcellus caps, then one line of EB Garamond: "Satu kawasan, banyak cerita." One hairline rule beneath. No navigation bar competing for the viewport. The primary action is a green plate — inline under the rule on desktop, fixed bottom-right on mobile. Preceded by a logo splash of about 1.4 seconds that draws the palm mark stroke by stroke, skippable on any input, shown once per session, replaced by an instant cut under prefers-reduced-motion.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION GRAMMAR: the breathing rooms are alive. As each section enters, the day writes itself in time order — one activity line at a time, exponential ease-out from an already-visible default, never from opacity zero. That single authored moment carries the page; every other element holds still. Full-bleed bands reveal by mask, not by fade. Motion honours prefers-reduced-motion by resolving to the finished state instantly.

FORM: the Program Hari Ini topology — the day laid out in time order — rendered in the user-pinned Maison Mastrorelli material world. Candidate 6 of the grounded list; seed key ccc70358, re-roll round 2. The pinned reference overrides the roll's materials; the day-as-spine ritual survives the translation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
